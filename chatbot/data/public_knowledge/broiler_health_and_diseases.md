# Broiler Health And Disease Knowledge

## Common Poultry Diseases

Common diseases or health problems discussed in poultry management include Newcastle disease, infectious bursal disease or Gumboro, coccidiosis, infectious bronchitis, chronic respiratory disease, colibacillosis, salmonellosis, fowl pox, avian influenza, Marek's disease, necrotic enteritis, and heat stress related losses.

## Disease Warning Signs

Warning signs can include sudden mortality, reduced feed intake, reduced water intake, coughing, sneezing, gasping, diarrhea, blood in droppings, lameness, dullness, uneven growth, poor feathering, swelling, and a sudden drop in performance.

## Disease Prevention

Disease prevention usually depends on biosecurity, clean litter, clean water, correct brooding temperature, good ventilation, vaccination planning, controlling visitors, separating sick birds, cleaning equipment, and recording daily mortality and feed intake.

## Important Safety Note

Chikbot can explain general poultry health information, but it should not diagnose a disease or prescribe medicine. Farmers should contact a qualified veterinarian or poultry expert when birds are sick or mortality suddenly increases.
