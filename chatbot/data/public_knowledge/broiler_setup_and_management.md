# Broiler Setup And Management

## Broiler Setup

A broiler setup usually needs a clean shed, dry litter, feeders, drinkers, brooding heat, ventilation, lighting, water supply, feed storage, disinfectants, and a plan for vaccination and daily checks.

## Daily Checks

Farmers should check bird activity, feed intake, water intake, mortality, litter condition, temperature, ventilation, sick birds, and average weight. Daily records make it easier to understand flock performance and detect problems early.

## Feed And Water

Feed and water should be clean and available according to bird age and farm management plan. Poor feed quality, water shortage, or dirty drinkers can reduce growth and increase health problems.

## Reports

Reports help farmers review daily data, costs, sales, mortality, current birds, feed usage, FCR, and profit or loss across the flock cycle.
