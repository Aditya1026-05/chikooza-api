# Poultry Basics

## What Is Poultry

Poultry means domesticated birds raised for meat, eggs, or breeding. Common poultry birds include chickens, ducks, turkeys, geese, and quail. In broiler farming, chickens are raised mainly for meat production.

## Broiler Farm Records

Broiler farmers should track the starting bird count, daily mortality, current birds, feed intake, feed stock, average bird weight, vaccination status, purchases, sales, and FCR. These records help the farmer understand growth, health, feed efficiency, and profit or loss.

## Feed Conversion Ratio

FCR means feed conversion ratio. It is commonly used to understand how efficiently birds convert feed into body weight. A lower FCR usually means better feed efficiency, but it must be interpreted with bird age, feed quality, breed, mortality, and management conditions.

## Mortality

Mortality means bird deaths during the flock cycle. Farmers should record daily mortality because sudden increases can indicate disease, heat stress, poor ventilation, water problems, feed problems, or management issues.

## Stocking Density

Stocking density means how many birds are kept in a shed area. It should consider bird age, breed, shed space, ventilation, litter condition, temperature, and local management guidelines.
