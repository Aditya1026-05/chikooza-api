# Chikooza Public Product Knowledge

## What Chikooza Does

Chikooza is a poultry farm records and operations platform. It helps farmers keep farm, flock, daily data, purchase, sale, and report records in one workspace instead of scattered notebooks or spreadsheets.

## Main Workspace Modules

- Farm: stores farm location, state, sheds, pin code, map point, and status.
- Flock: stores each broiler batch, hatch date, breed, bird count, chick cost, and farm assignment.
- Daily data: stores daily mortality, current birds, feed intake, feed stock, bird weight, FCR, vaccination, heating, cooling, and other checks.
- Purchases: stores feed, medicine, vaccination, equipment, labour, and other costs.
- Sales: stores partial or final lifting, birds sold, total weight, rate per kg, buyer details, and sale amount.
- Reports: exports daily data sheets and flock summary PDFs.

## Public Chikbot And Private AI Assistant

Chikbot on the public website explains Chikooza, poultry farm setup, broiler management, feed, mortality, FCR, and reports. It cannot access private farmer records. Logged-in users should use the AI Assistant inside the app for questions about their own saved farm data.

## Getting Started

A farmer starts by registering, verifying OTP, logging in, adding a farm, adding a flock, recording daily data, entering purchases, entering sales, and exporting reports.
